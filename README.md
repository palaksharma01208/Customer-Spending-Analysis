# Customer-Spending-Analysis
Analyzed customer spending using SQL - Joins, GROUP BY, CTEs and Views to find top-spending customers.
Key Insights: Palak is our top-spending customer with a total spend of ₹56,000, followed by Priya at ₹50,000. High-value products like Laptops drive most of the revenue, even with low quantity. Customers who buy high-ticket items once are more valuable than frequent low-value buyers. This shows the business should focus on retaining premium customers and promoting high-value products to increase revenue.
